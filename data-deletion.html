Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 17 May 2026

OpenClaw OS is a self-hosted application — your data lives on your own device(s), not on any OpenClaw server. Deleting your data is therefore fully under your control.

1. Revoke OAuth access

To stop OpenClaw OS from accessing your accounts, revoke the App's permissions:

  • Google (Gmail, Calendar): visit myaccount.google.com/permissions, find "OpenClaw OS" or "Sassyclaw", click Remove access.
  • Meta (Instagram): open Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites → Active → tap clawsaasy-IG → Remove.
  • Telegram: revoke the bot by messaging @BotFather with /deletebot, or simply block the bot in your Telegram chat.

2. Delete local data

Delete the OpenClaw OS data directory on the device(s) running it:

  1. Stop the OpenClaw services: launchctl unload ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.openclaw.*.plist
  2. Remove the data directory: rm -rf ~/.openclaw
  3. Confirm by checking the directory no longer exists: ls ~/.openclaw should report "No such file or directory".

3. Confirm with us

If you would like written confirmation that no residual data is held, email mittalkshitiz13@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Confirmation". We will respond within 30 days as required by GDPR.

4. Third-party data

To remove any AI processing logs at our model provider (OpenRouter), follow their data deletion process at openrouter.ai. OpenClaw OS does not control logs at third-party AI providers.
